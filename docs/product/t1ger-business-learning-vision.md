# T1GER Business Learning Vision

## Product thesis

T1GER should become an AI-guided education system that helps a person build a real business capability, not simply finish content. The product promise is measurable movement from understanding to execution:

**Learn -> Practice -> Apply -> Verify -> Reflect -> Adapt**

The current Investing vertical is the proving ground for this engine. T1GER Business can become the next major vertical once the core loop is reliable.

## Inspiration to adapt

- **Duolingo:** onboarding clarity, small decisions, visible progress, character-led feedback, and a daily habit. T1GER should adapt the interaction quality without copying Duolingo's visual identity or screen layouts.
- **Kinnu:** a navigable knowledge map, mastery rather than completion, active recall, and intelligent resurfacing of weak concepts. T1GER should connect that learning model to real-world missions.
- **100M Business:** an outcome-oriented business path covering offer, market, leads, sales, delivery, finance, operations, and AI. The name is a working product concept, not a promise of financial results.

## T1GER Business structure

The Business vertical should be a competency graph rather than one long course:

1. Choose a business outcome and current stage.
2. Diagnose gaps across the business competency graph.
3. Generate a short daily lesson and retrieval practice.
4. Assign one real action in the user's business.
5. Verify the action with connected data, an artifact, or structured reflection.
6. Update mastery, confidence, and the next recommended action.
7. Let the AI mentor explain tradeoffs using the user's actual context.

This combines Kinnu-like mastery with T1GER's differentiated Apply and verification layer.

## Affiliate and commission concept

An affiliate system can be a distribution layer for T1GER Business, but it should remain separate from learning progress:

- Commission is earned from valid subscription revenue, never from XP or leaderboard rank.
- Verified learning progress cannot be purchased or inflated through referrals.
- The product must show attribution, eligibility, pending commission, refunds, and payout status clearly.
- Referral messaging must not make income guarantees or imply a recruitment-based business model.
- A later creator track could reward experts for high-quality, evidence-backed missions, with editorial and safety review.

## AI advantage

AI should make the learning loop more personal and more accountable:

- infer the next useful concept from mastery and business stage;
- turn the learner's business data into examples and practice;
- provide feedback on artifacts such as offers, landing pages, call notes, and financial models;
- distinguish verified evidence from self-reported reflection;
- explain why a mission matters and what changed after completion;
- remember decisions and constraints so the user does not repeatedly re-explain the business.

The AI mentor is not the curriculum. It is the adaptive layer around a reviewed competency model, mission bank, verification policy, and safety rules.

## Product guardrails

- Keep Investing and Business as verticals on the same core engine, not separate apps.
- Do not promise wealth, revenue, or investment returns.
- Preserve one source of truth for verified XP.
- Reward execution quality and reflection, not screen time.
- Keep the mascot emotionally expressive but visually stable; reactions should change motion and posture, not the character's identity colour.
- Build a recognizable T1GER interaction language instead of a literal clone of another product.

## Recommended sequence

1. Finish the Investing onboarding and first-week learning loop.
2. Validate that Learn -> Apply -> Verify improves activation and seven-day retention.
3. Define the Business competency graph and one narrow outcome path.
4. Prototype AI adaptation with reviewed content and a transparent mastery model.
5. Add affiliate attribution only after subscription, refund, and analytics foundations are reliable.
