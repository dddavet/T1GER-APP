## Public product signals

- Kinnu reports 1.2 million learners, 3.2 million learning hours, and 90 million question sessions.
- Its stated north-star ambition is reducing “time to mastery.”
- Kinnu 2.0 organizes learning into cohesive standalone concept units called Orbs, arranged into increasingly storified pathways.
- Knowledge Bank, Memory Shields, and Memory Prediction expose what is retained versus at risk.
- Crane Stamps reward consistency without deleting progress when a day is missed.

## Published experimentation signals

Kinnu reports a program of 15 learning experiments with roughly 10,000 participants. The most relevant reported directional effects for T1GER are:

- Case studies: +82% K-Score.
- Orb pathways: +69%.
- Narrative pathways: +68%.
- Introductory questions and summary points: +70%.
- Concept Lookup: +66%.
- Why/How write-in questions: +66%.
- Collapsing Questions: +59%.
- Graph questions: +33%.

These percentages are Kinnu's own published experimental claims. They are useful product signals, not independent validation.

## T1GER interpretation

The reusable mechanism is not Kinnu's visual skin. It is the sequence: curiosity prime → connected narrative → compressed schema → varied retrieval → scheduled review. T1GER should preserve that mechanism while making verified execution the terminal condition.
